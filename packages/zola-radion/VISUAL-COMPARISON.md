# Visual adaptation

Pinned _vendor.scss, _theme.scss, post_components and TOC templates are the reference: monospace column, pink underlines, black/light canvas, date metadata, spaced summary entries and rounded folding TOC. Retained styles inherit Hack/After Dark with Radion’s own theme changes; acknowledgements and license texts are included.

Native text controls replace icons and DOM headings replace Zola’s precomputed TOC. The body remains 16 px on mobile. Installed Firekylin screenshots are in verification/zola-radion; no live upstream pixel comparison was performed.
