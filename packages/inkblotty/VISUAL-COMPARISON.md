# Visual adaptation

Upstream style.css/custom.css and header, summary and sidebar templates are the reference. The original white wrapper shadow, black menu, 1366 px maximum width, 75/25 columns, date/excerpt list, gray rules and red/blue article heading accents are retained.

Mobile links replace the original JavaScript-dependent select menu. The sidebar stacks at the source 956 px breakpoint. Images, icon fonts, WordPress-only controls and ads are omitted. Actual Firekylin desktop/mobile screenshots are recorded in verification/inkblotty; no live upstream pixel comparison was performed.
